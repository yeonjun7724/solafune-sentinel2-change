about=Detects and characterizes land-surface change between two Sentinel-2 acquisitions (B02/B03/B04) over a user-supplied AOI. Produces baseline and robust Change Vector Analysis rasters, change polygons, a GeoPackage results database, spatial autocorrelation statistics (Global/Local Moran's I, Getis-Ord Gi* with FDR correction), and an optional experimental unsupervised spatial-ML anomaly ranking. No ground-truth labels are used anywhere; results are exploratory and documented as such. Built for the Solafune Sentinel-2 Change Analysis technical assessment.
